# Storefront pricing requirements

Version 0.3, owned by the pricing team.

## Orders and discounts

R1. An order of 100.00 or more receives a 10 percent bulk discount.

R2. A coupon takes a fixed amount off the order. Percentage discounts are applied first, then the coupon.

R3. The receipt must be produced quickly and should be easy to read.

R4. Sales tax is charged at an appropriate rate, rounded as needed, and so on, etc.

R5. Totals are sent to the payment gateway in whole cents, exactly as shown on the receipt.

## Promotions

R6. The year-end promotion ends at 23:59:59 UTC on 31 December 2026.

## Coupons

R7. A customer may use at most one coupon per order.

R8. Customers may combine up to three coupons on one order.

## Notas de soporte (español)

R9. El sistema debe responder de forma rápida y ofrecer una experiencia amigable al cliente.

R10. El cupón se aplicará cuando sea posible, o según sea necesario.
